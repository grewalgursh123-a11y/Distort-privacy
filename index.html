Distort Privacy Policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

Overview

Distort is designed to create camera, LiDAR, depth, 3D, photo, and video effects on your device. The current version of Distort does not require an account and does not transmit your camera, LiDAR/depth, microphone, photo, or video content to Distort servers.

Camera & LiDAR / Depth

Distort uses the camera and, on supported devices, LiDAR/depth information to render real-time visual effects and create 3D scans. Camera images and depth information are processed on-device for these features. Distort does not upload this content to a Distort server.

Microphone

If you grant microphone permission, Distort uses the microphone to include audio in videos you choose to record. If microphone access is denied, video recording can continue without microphone audio. Microphone audio is processed on-device as part of the recording.

Photos & Videos

Distort requests add-only Photos permission so it can save photos and videos you create in the app to your Photo Library. The app does not use this permission to browse or read your existing Photo Library. Saved media remains under your control in the Photos app.

Data Collection & Sharing

The current version of Distort does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, user accounts, or cross-app tracking. Distort does not sell personal information. Distort does not share your camera, LiDAR/depth, microphone, photo, or video content with third parties through the app.

Retention & Deletion

Distort may create temporary local files while a recording is being completed and saved. Media you choose to save is stored in your Photo Library. You can delete saved photos or videos at any time in Photos. Because the current version does not maintain a Distort account or server-side user-content database, there is no Distort account data or uploaded media to delete from a Distort server.

Your Choices

You can revoke Camera, Microphone, or Photos permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Features that require a permission may stop working when that permission is disabled.

Changes to This Policy

If Distort adds new data practices, online services, analytics, advertising, accounts, or other features that change how information is handled, this policy will be updated to describe those changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact provided for Distort on its App Store listing.
