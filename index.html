Distort Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Overview

Distort is a camera, LiDAR, depth, 3D, photo and video effects app for iPhone. Distort does not require an account. Your camera, LiDAR/depth, microphone, photo, video and tracking content is processed on your device rather than uploaded to a Distort server.

Camera, LiDAR, TrueDepth & Depth

Distort uses camera images and, on supported devices, LiDAR, TrueDepth and ARKit tracking information to render live visual effects and 3D scenes. Supported effects can estimate depth on-device when hardware depth is unavailable. Front-camera processing may use ARKit face tracking. This information is used for visual effects, not to identify you, and is not sent to external AI services.

TrueDepth and Face Data — Information Accessed

When you use supported front-camera effects, Distort accesses camera images and TrueDepth depth maps (distances from the camera to visible surfaces, which may include your face). ARKit face-tracking sessions provide the front-camera capture context and camera tracking used by these effects. The app uses image and depth buffers; it does not read ARFaceAnchor face-mesh geometry, expression blend-shape coefficients or eye-gaze values. Distort does not access Face ID authentication templates or use face data to identify or authenticate people.

TrueDepth and Face Data — Purpose and Use

Face-containing images and depth information are processed on your device to create live camera effects, colored point clouds, 3D scans, subject masks and creative recordings. These same local processing practices apply to LiDAR, Cube, Time Block, 3D, Body Tracking, Color Scatter, Glitch Mix, Echo, Warp and Voxel, including subject cutouts, retained scans, simulations, portals and placed elements. Distort does not use face data for advertising, profiling, identification, analytics or training AI models.

TrueDepth and Face Data — Sharing and Storage

Distort does not upload face images, TrueDepth maps or face-derived scans to Distort servers or automatically disclose them to third parties. Processing and working buffers stay on the device. If you record, save or export a creation, it may contain a visible face or a 3D representation derived from face depth. 3D point-cloud exports are written to the app Documents/3D Scans folder; 3D video recordings are written to Documents/3D Videos. Other recording and import workflows use local temporary files. Saving to Photos or sharing through the system share sheet creates copies at the destination you choose. Apple backup or Photos synchronization may store eligible copies according to your device settings; a sharing destination handles its copy under its own privacy practices.

TrueDepth and Face Data — Retention and Deletion

Live image and depth buffers are replaced during processing. Captured scans and subject cutouts can stay in memory while you work or navigate simulations, until cleared or reset, their owning session is released, or the app process ends. Stopping a scan or switching simulations does not necessarily clear retained scans. Face-derived data included in a saved image, video or point-cloud export persists with that creation. Local Documents exports and recordings have no automatic time-based deletion schedule; deleting the app removes its local app data (offloading the app does not). Delete separately saved copies in Photos, Files or the service you shared them with, including Recently Deleted where applicable. Temporary import and recording files are cleaned up by relevant workflows or the operating system, but interrupted sessions can leave files behind. Backup copies follow your backup provider settings. Distort has no server-side face-data store to delete.

3D Scans & Device Motion

3D features combine images, depth and motion tracking to create local point clouds and scene reconstructions. These may depict people, objects and surroundings you scan. Device motion helps orient 3D previews and recordings. You choose when to save or export your creations.

On-Device AI & Body Tracking

Distort uses bundled models including Depth Anything V2 Small and DETR, along with Apple Vision, to estimate depth, detect subjects and objects, analyze body and hand poses and color scenes. These features run on-device without sending frames to an external AI service. AI-generated results may be inaccurate and are intended for creative effects, not precise measurements or safety-critical uses.

Microphone

With your permission, Distort uses your microphone to include audio when you record video. Audio is processed on-device. If microphone permission is denied, supported video recording can continue without audio.

Imported Photos, Videos & Saving

Import Photo and Import Video use Apple's system picker to access only the media you select. Selected media and derived depth or subject masks are processed and temporarily cached on-device for editing; they are not uploaded. Distort requests add-only Photos permission to save creations and does not use that permission to browse your entire library.

If you choose to share an exported creation, the service you select through the iOS share sheet handles that copy under its own privacy practices. Media saved to Photos may sync through your Apple services according to your device settings.

Distort Pro Subscriptions

Distort Pro unlocks recording and saving in the LiDAR and Cube modes. Apple processes subscription payments, offer codes and cancellations. Distort uses Apple-verified purchase and entitlement status on-device to unlock these features. Distort does not receive your payment card information or send your purchase data to a Distort server. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Data Collection & Sharing

In builds that include the Meta SDK, Distort requests iOS tracking permission before initializing the SDK. If you allow tracking, Distort reports app openings to Meta with associated app and device information so Meta can attribute installs and activations to Distort ads and report campaign performance. Meta receives the network IP address when the SDK connects. Distort disables automatic event logging and SDK collection of the iOS advertising identifier. Camera features work if you decline. You can withdraw permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. This stops future activation reporting by Distort. Previously sent events are subject to Meta’s data retention and deletion practices. For questions about previously sent data, contact Distort Support or visit Meta’s Privacy Policy at https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/. Distort does not sell personal information or send face data, scans, camera images, recordings, imported media or body-tracking results to Meta.

Retention & Deletion

Camera frames, analysis masks, pose/object results, scans and temporary recording files remain on-device as needed for the active effect or editing session. Imported-media copies and analysis caches are scheduled for deletion when replaced or when you leave editing. Temporary files from interrupted sessions may remain until cleaned up by the app or operating system. Creations you save remain in Photos, Files or your selected destination until you delete them there. Distort does not maintain a server-side user-content library.

Your Choices

You can manage Camera, Microphone, Photos and Motion permissions in iPhone Settings. Disabling permissions may prevent features that rely on them from working. You choose what to import, record, save and share.

Changes to This Policy

If Distort adds data practices or online services that change how your information is handled, we will update this policy.
