About Distort

Distort is a camera and LiDAR visual-effects app for iPhone. It includes real-time and capture-based effects such as LiDAR scanning, Voxel, Wrap, Color Scatter, 3D Scan, and other experimental visual modes.

Device Requirements

Some Distort features require an iPhone or iPad equipped with a LiDAR Scanner. Features that rely on depth sensing may be unavailable or behave differently on devices without LiDAR hardware.

Camera, Microphone & Photos

  • Camera access is required for camera and LiDAR effects.
  • Microphone access is required when recording video with audio.
  • Photos access is used when saving photos and videos to your device.

You can review these permissions in iPhone Settings → Distort.

Troubleshooting

  • Close and reopen Distort if the camera preview or an effect stops responding.
  • Make sure Camera, Microphone, and Photos permissions are enabled.
  • For LiDAR modes, use a supported LiDAR-equipped device.
  • Keep enough free storage available when recording or processing scans.
  • If a saved video does not appear immediately, reopen the Photos app and check Recents.

Contact Support

If you still need help, email Distort support and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.
